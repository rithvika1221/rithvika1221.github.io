About me

I like problems that sit where technology meets real people — a student who can't find their lost backpack, a teenager who needs a mental-health resource, a local shop that deserves more than a star rating.

Most of my projects are full-stack Next.js apps backed by PostgreSQL. I've also gone deeper into how databases behave under load: my research project measures and predicts logical-replication lag in PostgreSQL. Along the way I've sharpened my algorithms through USACO and Java/C++ practice.

Research · Databases

Predicting Logical Replication Lag in PostgreSQL

An independent research study of how PostgreSQL logical replication behaves under changing write throughput and network latency. I built a repeatable experiment matrix, measured publisher-side cost (CPU, WAL generation, commit latency) and subscriber lag over time, and used the data to train models that predict lag for early warning and capacity planning.
